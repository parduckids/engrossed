# Privacy Policy for Engrossed

**Last updated: 27 October 2026**

## The short version

Engrossed does not collect anything. It has no account system and no servers of its own.

The only network activity in the app is Apple's own App Store, used to offer and restore the
one-time purchase, and ElevenLabs, but only if you choose to add your own ElevenLabs key for
read aloud (see below). Your library, your reading history and your place in your books are never
sent anywhere.

## What is stored, and where

Everything Engrossed keeps is stored only on your own device, inside the app's own folder:

- **The PDFs you import.** When you add a PDF, Engrossed copies it into its own storage so it can
  read it later. The original file on your device is not modified or moved.
- **The text it extracts from them,** cached so that reopening a long book is fast.
- **Your place in each book**, along with how long you have spent reading and the few words that
  were on screen when you stopped.
- **Your reading preferences**, such as text size, theme and card size.
- **An ElevenLabs API key, only if you add one.** It is kept in your device's keychain, stays on
  that device, and is never synced to iCloud.

None of this is transmitted anywhere, apart from that key, which goes only to ElevenLabs as
described below. It is not backed up to any service operated by us, because there is no such
service. If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your device's own
backup may include this data, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Deleting the app removes all of it. The one exception is an ElevenLabs key: the system keychain can
keep it after the app is gone, so use Remove key in Settings first if you want it deleted.

## Optional ElevenLabs voices

Read aloud normally uses your device's own built-in voice, and nothing leaves your device. If you
want, you can paste in your own ElevenLabs API key to use ElevenLabs voices instead. Only then, and
only while an ElevenLabs voice is reading, the text of each card being read is sent to ElevenLabs
over an encrypted connection, together with your key, so that ElevenLabs can turn it into speech.
To avoid a pause between cards, the next card is sent a moment before it is read. Nothing else is
sent: not the rest of the book, not your place in it, and nothing about you. That text is handled by
ElevenLabs under your own agreement with them and their privacy policy, not ours. We never see it.
Remove the key in Settings and nothing more is sent.

## What is not collected

No personal information. No email address, name, or contact details. No analytics, telemetry,
crash reporting, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, or location. No cookies. No tracking
of any kind, and no third-party SDKs that could do any of it.

## Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your
payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists. Apple's handling of that
transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Children

Engrossed is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the app collects nothing,
any change would most likely be to say so more clearly.
