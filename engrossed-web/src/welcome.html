Engrossed

Read long articles without losing focus.

That page is one of Chrome's own, and Chrome doesn't let any extension run there. Open an article and try again.

Chrome's PDF viewer doesn't let extensions in. For PDFs and books, the Engrossed app for iPhone, iPad and Mac does the same thing, and remembers your place there too.

HOW TO USE IT

  1. Open an article: a blog post, a news story, a documentation page.
  2. Click the Engrossed button in the toolbar, or press ⌘⇧E.
  3. Click the text or press Space for the next few sentences. Esc to close.
Try it on an article

If you can't see the button, click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin Engrossed. Your place is saved for every article, so you can close it and come back tomorrow.
