## Description
```
Long articles are hard to stay with. Engrossed is a free Chrome extension that shows a web article a few sentences at a time, with nothing else on the screen. Click, or press Space, for the next few. Or read the whole article as one page, with everything except your place dimmed.

Your place is saved in every article, so you can close it and carry on later from the same sentence. Longer articles have a short check-in at the end of each part, where you say in one line what you just read. There is also an optional reading timer, a list of the article's sections, read aloud, and four themes: Paper, Sepia, Slate and Night.

It is free, with no account and no tracking, and it only runs when you click its button. Made with ADHD and autistic readers in mind.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store:
https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/engrossed/bopoanglnpekmepeeaofdofoealcngdg

Website:
https://parduckids.github.io/engrossed/chrome.html

An Engrossed app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, for PDFs and ebooks, is coming soon, with no subscription.

Voice and music made with ElevenLabs.
```

## Upload steps

1. Go to studio.youtube.com and sign in with the account you want the video on.
2. Click **Create** (top right) > **Upload videos**, and pick `store/promo-video.mp4`.
3. **Details** page:
   - Paste the title and the description from above.
   - **Thumbnail** > **Upload file** > pick `store/youtube-thumbnail.png`.
     If the button is greyed out, the account is not verified yet: open youtube.com/verify in a
     new tab, verify with a phone number (takes a minute, the code comes by text), then come back
     and refresh the upload page. The upload itself keeps going.
   - **Audience**: No, it's not made for kids.
   - Click **Show more**: set Altered content to Yes, paste the tags, set the language,
     category, comments and "Allow embedding" as in the table above.
4. **Video elements**: nothing needed. Click Next.
5. **Checks**: wait for it to finish (a copyright check; the music was made for this video with ElevenLabs, so it should pass). Click Next.
6. **Visibility**: Public (or Unlisted). Click **Publish**.
7. Copy the video link it shows (looks like `https://youtu.be/...`).

If you upload before verifying, you can add the thumbnail later: Studio > Content > click the
video > Thumbnail > Upload file > Save.

## Put the video on the Chrome Web Store page

1. Open the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard and click **Engrossed**.
2. Go to **Store listing** and scroll to **Promo video**.
3. Paste the YouTube link, then click **Save draft**.
4. Click **Submit for review**. The video shows on the store page once the review passes.

Version 1.0.1 is prepared but not uploaded yet. If you are uploading it today too, upload the
package on the **Package** tab first, then add the video, and submit once so both go through
the same review.
