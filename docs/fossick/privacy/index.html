Fossick: Privacy Policy

For the app on iPhone. Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version. Fossick does not collect, store or transmit personal data. It has no account system and no server of its own. Your fossicks, your finds and your photos never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. The only request it makes on its own is for the weather, with a location rounded to about 10 km, and you can turn that off.

Your fossicks, finds and photos

Everything you enter is stored in the app's own folder on your device and nowhere else. It is included in your normal iPhone backup to iCloud or a Mac, which Apple handles under Apple's privacy policy, not ours. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location when you ask it to pin where you stand, and when the list sorts fossicks by distance. It is read while the app is in use, never tracked in the background, and not stored beyond the pins you choose to make.

The map

Map tiles come from Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy. Your pins are drawn by the app on top of the map and are never sent to Apple.

Photos

Photos you attach are copied into the app at a reduced size. The originals in your photo library are not changed.

If you ask Fossick to find your spots in your photos, and allow it access to all of your photos or to an album you choose, it reads where and when each photo was taken and looks at small copies with Apple's image recognition, on the phone, to spot fish, mushrooms, berries and the like. Places you are at most days, screenshots and indoor photos are skipped. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is kept except the places and photos you choose to keep. You can change or remove the app's access in iPhone Settings at any time.

Voice notes

The microphone is used only while you hold a recording, and the recording is kept as a small file beside the log. The words are written down by the phone's own speech recognition, on the device, with no internet. If the phone cannot do that for your language, the recording is kept and nothing is sent anywhere.

Weather

The weather and tide recorded with your finds, and the rain since your last visit, come from Open-Meteo, a free weather service that needs no account. To get the weather, the app sends Open-Meteo a location rounded to about 10 km and, for past finds, the dates. It never sends a fossick, a find, a name or a photo. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy covers those requests. Turn Weather off in the app's Settings and no weather requests are made.

Sharing

A fossick you choose to send travels as a file through AirDrop, Messages or whatever you pick, with its finds and reduced copies of their photos, but without voice recordings. The photo copies carry no camera or location data. It goes only where you send it, and through no server of ours.

Holding two phones together sends a fossick directly between them over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with no internet. The app looks for the other phone only while that screen is open.

Backups and exports

Backup, CSV and GPX files are made on the phone when you tap and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where they go after that is your choice.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists.

Notifications

Season reminders are scheduled on the device by the app itself. No notification service is involved.

What is not collected

No personal information. No email address, name or contact details. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, usage statistics, advertising identifiers or cookies. No tracking of any kind, and no third-party code that could do any of it.

Children

Fossick is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
