Possie

Your secret spots, kept secret. An app for iPhone.

A possie is a good spot: the rock ledge that fires on the run-out tide, the pine forest where the milk caps come up after rain, the blackberries nobody else has noticed. Possie is a private map of yours, with what you found there and when. No account, no cloud, no one else's server.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: parduckids@gmail.com. Your data never leaves your phone, so if something goes wrong a backup file from Settings is the thing to keep safe before you try anything else.
