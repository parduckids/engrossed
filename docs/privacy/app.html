Engrossed: Privacy Policy

For the app on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version. Engrossed does not collect anything. It has no account system and no servers of its own. Your library, your reading history and your place in your books are never sent anywhere.

The only network activity in the app is Apple's own App Store, used to offer and restore the one-time purchase, and ElevenLabs, but only if you choose to add your own ElevenLabs key for read aloud.

What is stored, and where

Everything Engrossed keeps is stored only on your own device, inside the app's own folder:

  • The books you import, as PDF or EPUB. Engrossed copies each one into its own storage so it can read it later. The original file on your device is not modified or moved.
  • The text it extracts from them, cached so that reopening a long book is fast.
  • Your place in each book, along with how long you have spent reading and the few words that were on screen when you stopped.
  • Your reading preferences, such as text size, theme and how much text is shown at a time.
  • An ElevenLabs API key, only if you add one. It is kept in your device's keychain, stays on that device, and is never synced to iCloud.

None of this is transmitted anywhere, apart from that key, which goes only to ElevenLabs as described below. It is not backed up to any service operated by us, because there is no such service. If you have iCloud Backup turned on, your device's own backup may include this data, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Deleting the app removes all of it. The one exception is an ElevenLabs key: the system keychain can keep it after the app is gone, so use Remove key in Settings first if you want it deleted.

Optional ElevenLabs voices

Read aloud normally uses your device's own built-in voice, and nothing leaves your device. If you want, you can paste in your own ElevenLabs API key to use ElevenLabs voices instead.

Only then, and only while an ElevenLabs voice is reading, the few sentences being read are sent to ElevenLabs over an encrypted connection, together with your key, so that ElevenLabs can turn them into speech. To avoid a pause, the next few sentences are sent a moment before they are read. Nothing else is sent: not the rest of the book, not your place in it, and nothing about you.

That text is handled by ElevenLabs under your own agreement with them and their privacy policy, not ours. We never see it. Remove the key in Settings and nothing more is sent.

What is not collected

No personal information. No email address, name or contact details. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, usage statistics, advertising identifiers or location. No cookies. No tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDKs that could do any of it.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Engrossed is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.
