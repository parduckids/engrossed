Daymark

Sun, moon and tide, offline. An app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Daymark tells you what the sky and the sea are doing today, wherever you are: sunrise, golden hour, the Moon's phase as it looks from where you stand, and the next high tide at the nearest station. All of it is computed on the device from astronomical formulas and bundled tide constants, so it works on the beach, on the water and on a sand track with no signal at all.
