Daymark: Privacy Policy

For the app on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version. Daymark does not collect, store or transmit personal data. It has no account system and no server of its own. Everything it shows is computed on your device.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location once each time it opens, at roughly one-kilometre accuracy, to work out sunrise, sunset, moon times and the tide at the nearest station. The last fix is kept on the device so the app has something to show before the next one. It is never sent anywhere. Watch complications may read location the same way, under the permission you gave the watch app. Location is read only while the app or a complication is in use, never tracked in the background.

Place names and search

The name shown for your current location comes from Apple's reverse geocoder, and adding a place by name uses Apple's local search. In both cases the only thing sent is the coordinate or the text you typed, to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Places you save are stored on the device.

Tide, sun and moon data

Tide predictions use harmonic constants published by NOAA's Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services, and constants derived from hourly observations published by the Australian Bureau of Meteorology's Baseline Sea Level Monitoring Project and by the IOC Sea Level Station Monitoring Facility. All of it is bundled inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you use it.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists.

What is not collected

No personal information. No email address, name or contact details. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, usage statistics, advertising identifiers or cookies. No tracking of any kind, and no third-party code that could do any of it.

Children

Daymark is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
