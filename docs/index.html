Engrossed

Read long texts without losing focus: a few sentences at a time, with your place saved.

Made for anyone whose mind wanders while reading. Engrossed shows a book or an article a few sentences at a time, with nothing else on the screen, and remembers exactly where you stopped.

The Engrossed app for iPhone, iPad and Mac is coming soon.
