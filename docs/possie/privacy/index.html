Possie: Privacy Policy

For the app on iPhone. Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version. Possie does not collect, store or transmit personal data. It has no account system and no server of its own. Your possies, your finds and your photos never leave your phone unless you share them yourself.

Your possies, finds and photos

Everything you enter is stored in the app's own folder on your device and nowhere else. It is included in your normal iPhone backup to iCloud or a Mac, which Apple handles under Apple's privacy policy, not ours. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location when you ask it to pin where you stand, and when the list sorts possies by distance. It is read while the app is in use, never tracked in the background, and not stored beyond the pins you choose to make.

The map

Map tiles come from Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy. Your pins are drawn by the app on top of the map and are never sent to Apple.

Photos

Photos you attach are chosen with the system photo picker and copied into the app at a reduced size. The originals in your photo library are not changed, and the app has no access to the rest of your library.

Backups and exports

Backup, CSV and GPX files are made on the phone when you tap and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where they go after that is your choice.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists.

Notifications

Season reminders are scheduled on the device by the app itself. No notification service is involved.

What is not collected

No personal information. No email address, name or contact details. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, usage statistics, advertising identifiers or cookies. No tracking of any kind, and no third-party code that could do any of it.

Children

Possie is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
